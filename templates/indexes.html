Without Indexes (COLLSCAN)

• Collection Scan reads every document from disk sequentially.

• Time complexity: O(N) where N = total documents.

• High CPU and I/O usage, query speed degrades rapidly as the gym scales to thousands of members.

• Cannot enforce uniqueness constraints (e.g. duplicate member emails allowed).

With Indexes (IXSCAN)

• B-Tree Index Scan navigates directly to the target leaf nodes.

• Time complexity: O(log N) logarithmic search.

• idx_member_date_compound sorts results directly in index order, avoiding in-memory sort.

• idx_email_unique enforces instantaneous uniqueness check at write time.
